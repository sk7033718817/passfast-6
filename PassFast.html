  • Voice sunne ke liye browser apni speech service use kar sakta hai.

  • Recovery ke liye vault ki ek encrypted copy sirf aapke phone me rakhi jati hai. Use kholne ki chabi sirf us mobile number ke OTP se milti hai.
  • OTP sirf aapke diye mobile number par SMS se aata hai aur 10 minute me expire ho jata hai.
  • Recovery chalu kiye bina Master Password bhoolne par data wapas nahi aa sakta.
  • Jarvis AI baatcheet ON ho to jo sawal aap Jarvis se poochte ho (sirf wo text) hamare server ke raaste AI service (Anthropic) tak jata hai. Passwords, usernames aur account ke naam kabhi nahi bheje jate. Settings me ise band kar sakte ho.
